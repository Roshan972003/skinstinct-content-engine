# Brand & audience context (Skinstinct)

Facts below come from the case narrative and the voice reference
(`../../voice-skill.txt`). Do not add facts here that are not sourced from
one of those two places — this file is loaded into every AI call as ground
truth, so anything wrong here becomes a wrong claim in every draft.

## Founder
- Meera Pillai, founder of Skinstinct.
- Two years in pharmaceutical formulation (stability review/documentation)
  before going independent.
- Launched Skinstinct 18 months ago in Mumbai.
- Not a dermatologist, no medical degree — she says this herself and it is
  part of her credibility, not a gap to paper over.

## Brand
- D2C skincare, minimal-ingredient formulations.
- Revenue: ₹14–16 lakh/month.
- 6,200 LinkedIn followers.
- Voice differentiator: precise, evidence-based language — pH values,
  active concentrations, delivery/stability data — instead of vague
  wellness language.
- All products fragrance-free (deliberate, sensitisation-risk decision).
- Flagship niacinamide serum: pH 5.5–5.8, documented per batch.
- No Vitamin C product yet (stability unsolved as of the source material).
- No peptide product yet.
- Publishes testing/sourcing detail (sample size, study type, who ran it)
  rather than unqualified claims.

## Audience
- Urban women, 28–40.
- Explicitly "tired of being sold to."
- Respond to founders who demonstrate real scientific literacy, not
  marketing language.

## What already works (do not contradict)
- Her last published post (a breakdown of why niacinamide *concentration*
  matters more than the label percentage) drove 340 profile visits in 48
  hours and 3 wholesale enquiries.
- Her 4 published LinkedIn posts have 47,000 combined impressions.
- A hired writer's posts were accurate but off-voice; she rewrote them more
  than she would have written from scratch and stopped after 4 posts. This
  is the failure mode this workflow must not repeat — voice fidelity is not
  optional.
